This notice explains how Foreverlikethemoon handles your personal information, as the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) requires.

## Who we are

Foreverlikethemoon is a sole trader operated by Henrike Nel, Villa Italia, Century City, 7441, Cape Town, South Africa. We are the responsible party for the information described here. Contact: foreverlikethemoon@gmail.com or WhatsApp +27 72 035 1440.

## What we collect

When you place an order:

- your name and email address
- your WhatsApp number, if you choose to give it
- your delivery address, for physical products only
- any notes you add to the order
- what you ordered, the amounts, and the payment and delivery status of the order

When you use the order form, Cloudflare Turnstile checks that you are a person and not an automated script. To do that it processes technical information about your browser and your IP address.

We do not collect card details. Payments are made by EFT or SnapScan, outside this website.

## Why we use it

- to process your order, send your invoice and deliver your product
- to match your payment to your order
- to contact you about your order
- to keep the business records South African tax law requires
- to protect the order form from abuse

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for marketing unless you ask us to.

## Who processes it for us

We use a small number of service providers to run the shop. Each processes your information only to provide its service to us:

- **Cloudflare** hosts this website, runs the order system and the Turnstile check
- **Google** stores order records in an access-controlled spreadsheet
- **Resend** sends order emails

Some of these providers store information outside South Africa. We use them because they apply protection comparable to POPIA.

## How long we keep it

We keep order records for five years, because tax law requires business records to be kept for that long. After that we delete them.

## Your rights

You may ask us to show you the information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it where we are not required to keep it. You may also object to how we use it. Send your request to foreverlikethemoon@gmail.com.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you may complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa at [inforegulator.org.za](https://inforegulator.org.za).

## Cookies

This website does not set its own tracking or advertising cookies. The Turnstile check on the order form may set a cookie that it needs in order to work.
