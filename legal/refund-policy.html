Refund policy

7-day cooling-off period

You may cancel your order and receive a refund within 7 days, without giving a reason and without penalty.

Physical products

For the Wedding Planner and the Wedding Emergency Kit, the 7 days start on the day you receive the goods. Tell us within the 7 days that you are cancelling, then return the goods. The only cost you may be charged is the direct cost of returning the goods. Once we have the goods back, any amount you paid for them is refunded.

Digital Wedding Planner

For the Digital Wedding Planner, the 7 days start on the day you place your order. Once you have downloaded the Digital Wedding Planner, your cooling-off right for that digital item ends, because the download cannot be returned. If you have not downloaded it, you may cancel within the 7 days and receive a refund.
