# Refund policy

We want you to be happy with what you order. If something isn't right, tell us and we'll sort it out.

## Digital products (e.g. the Digital Wedding Planner)

Because a digital file can't be returned once it's been delivered, we don't offer refunds simply because you've changed your mind. We will refund you, or send a working replacement, if:

- the file is faulty, damaged or won't open
- it doesn't match what we described on the product page
- we're unable to deliver it to you

Please tell us within 5 days of receiving it, and let us know what's wrong so we can fix it quickly.

## Physical products (e.g. printed planners and kits)

If your order arrives damaged, faulty or not as described, contact us within 3 days of receiving it and include a photo. We'll replace the item or refund you, whichever you prefer. Please keep the item and its packaging until we've replied.

## Made-to-order and custom work (e.g. wedding stationery, wedding websites, digital designs)

These are created specially for you, so they can't be refunded once work has started, except where the finished work is faulty or doesn't match what we agreed in writing. For wedding websites, the payment and cancellation terms in our Wedding Website Terms apply.

## How to ask for a refund

Email foreverlikethemoon@gmail.com or WhatsApp +27 72 035 1440. Include your order reference and a short description of the problem. We'll reply within 5 business days.

## How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are paid by EFT to the account provided by the customer, within 5 business days of approval.

## Your legal rights

Nothing in this policy limits your rights under South African consumer law.
